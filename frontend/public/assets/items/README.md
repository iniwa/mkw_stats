The source has not established probability percentages or distance thresholds. Time boundaries are approximate. It reports Golden Dash Mushroom from about 30 seconds and Lightning from about 40 seconds; initial availability of Kamek and Spiny Shell remains under investigation. Do not infer exact probabilities from these images or assume the 26-second table unlocks all of its listed items immediately.

The old `item-table-24p.png` and `item-table-12p.png` are v1.6.0 reference assets retained for history only, with no live UI references. Use the versioned phase assets for the current display. Do not infer rules for other player counts, changing Knockout Tour populations, or custom item settings from the combined 12/24-player charts.
